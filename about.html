About RYNOR

A shared purpose.
A considered path forward.

RYNOR Medtech is developing a peritoneal dialysis machine and supporting supplies, with a focus on the next stage of clinical evaluation.

Our purpose

We are working towards a future where peritoneal dialysis technology and its supporting supplies are considered together, around the people who will use them.

A complete perspective

Our development plans bring the machine, dialysis fluid bags, tubing, catheters and accessories into one coordinated future range.

A responsible progression

Preparing for clinical trials is our current priority. Verified progress, clinical evaluation and regulatory milestones will guide the path to launch.

An open conversation

We welcome questions and conversations with clinicians, hospitals, patients, distributors and prospective investors.

A few clear answers

Where we are today.

Is the RYNOR machine available to buy?

No. The machine is in development and we are preparing for clinical trials. Commercial availability has not been announced.

Can I order the supplies separately now?

The entire supply range is planned to launch together with the machine. None of the products shown are currently offered for sale.

Do the images show the final products?

The product images on this website are concept illustrations. The final appearance, packaging and specifications may change during development.

How can I contact the team?
