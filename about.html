A few clear answers

Where we are today.

Is the RYNOR machine available to buy?

No. The machine is in development and we are preparing for clinical trials. Commercial availability has not been announced.

Can I order the supplies separately now?

No. CAPD fluid bags and tubing are not currently available. Contact us for future availability updates. Transfer sets have a separate availability enquiry. The APD machine and its dedicated range remain in development.

Do the images show the final products?

Supply images are technical illustrations, not photographs of stock. Ask us for the actual product and pack details. The APD machine is a development concept and its final design may change.

How can I contact the team?

Email syedkamran@rynormedtech.com, or use the enquiry button to send us a message.
