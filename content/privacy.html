How RYNOR uses information submitted through interest and enquiry forms.

Notice version: PD interest · 19 October 2026

Who you are contacting

RYNOR Medtech. For questions about this notice, stopping contact or requesting deletion, email syedkamran@rynormedtech.com.

What the forms collect

Supply and general enquiries collect either your phone number or email, an optional name, the selected topic and your message. We use them to respond to your request, including product prices and availability. Supply enquiries are labelled separately from PD interest registrations. Please do not include medical records or sensitive health information in a message.

The PD interest form collects your name, whether you are a patient/caregiver or doctor/PD professional, and one contact method: phone or email. You may also provide a city, preferred language and, for professionals, an organisation. We record your consent choice, the notice version and submission timing. Development-update consent is optional and separate.

Please use your own contact details. Do not submit diagnoses, medical records, identity documents or another person’s contact information. The form is for adults; a parent or guardian should contact us for someone under 18.

Why we use it

To respond to your interest in PD education and RYNOR’s development programme. If you separately opt in, we may also send occasional development updates. Interest registration does not provide medical care, determine treatment suitability, make a purchase or enrol you in a clinical trial.

Where submissions go

The form sends information over HTTPS to RYNOR’s existing Formspree account. Authorised RYNOR account users can handle these submissions; notification emails and archive availability depend on account settings and the service plan. Contact details are not published on this website or stored in its public source repository.

Formspree provides the form-processing service and states that its infrastructure is hosted in the United States. See Formspree’s privacy policy and security information. Standard hosting and service logs may contain technical request information.

Your choices

Only provide information you want to share. We use it to handle your enquiry and any updates you explicitly requested, and keep it only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. You can withdraw contact or update consent, correct your details, or request deletion by emailing syedkamran@rynormedtech.com. Use “PD interest privacy request” as the subject and tell us what you want us to do.

Learning activities and video

The learning answers stay in the page’s memory and are not attached to your interest submission. The educational video is hosted with this website rather than an embedded advertising platform. The site may remember your existing motion preference in browser storage. You can read the educational content without submitting the form.
