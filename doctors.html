Clinician questions

Clear answers.

Is the RYNOR APD machine available for clinical use?

No. It is preparing for clinical trials and is not available for sale or clinical use. Commercial availability will follow evaluation and regulatory steps.

Which supplies can my centre order now?

None at present. All supply categories are currently not available. Use Notify me in the supplies catalogue to hear first when they can be ordered.

How are trial sites selected?

Details will be shared when the clinical evaluation plan is confirmed. Registering interest helps us contact you first.

Who can I speak to?

Use the collaboration form or WhatsApp, and our team will connect you with the right person.
