Redefining life on dialysis.

The Rynor Pleasant is coming to ease the burden of end-stage renal disease, bringing safe, affordable, and seamless therapy directly to your home.

The Market Gap
Why is PD rare in India?

Historically, Peritoneal Dialysis (PD) has been a luxury reserved for the few due to prohibitively expensive imported machines and monopolized supplies. Rynor Medtech is breaking this barrier by manufacturing a world-class, indigenous solution designed specifically for the modern Indian patient.

Absolute Freedom

Designed for life outside the hospital.

  • Therapy While You Sleep: The machine quietly performs dialysis over 8 hours at night, leaving your days completely free.
  • Travel Anywhere: A lightweight, highly portable design. Take your treatment with you to visit family or go on vacation.
  • Uninterrupted Care: Built-in battery operation ensures life-sustaining therapy never stops, even during a power outage.
  • Ease of Use: A cassette-free system that operates on standard fluid bags available from any authorized Rynor distributor.

The Health Benefits

A gentler, more natural approach to care.

  • Needle-Free Comfort: Escape the pain, anxiety, and vascular access issues of traditional in-center hemodialysis.
  • Gentle on the Heart: Slower, daily dialysis mimics natural kidney function, resulting in significantly less stress on the cardiovascular system.
  • Dietary Independence: Continuous cleaning often allows patients to enjoy a less restrictive diet and greater fluid intake.
