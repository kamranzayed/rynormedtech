Understanding dialysis at home

RYNOR’s machine and supplies are in development and not yet available for purchase.

Peritoneal dialysis can allow suitable patients to receive treatment at home. After a procedure to place a soft tube called a catheter in the abdomen, patients receive training to manage treatment themselves or with a caregiver. With ongoing support from their kidney-care team, many people can continue working and taking part in everyday activities.

Your kidney-care team will guide treatment suitability, diet, fluid limits and support needs.
