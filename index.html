The Transformation
Designed for Life Outside the Hospital

Absolute Freedom

Engineered for portability and peace of mind.

  • Therapy While You Sleep: The machine quietly performs dialysis over 8 hours at night, leaving your days completely free.
  • Travel Anywhere: A lightweight, highly portable design. Take your treatment with you seamlessly.
  • Uninterrupted Care: Built-in battery operation ensures therapy never stops during a power outage.

The Health Benefits

A gentler, more natural approach to care.

  • Needle-Free Comfort: Escape the pain, anxiety, and vascular access issues of traditional in-center hemodialysis.
  • Gentle on the Heart: Slower, daily dialysis mimics natural kidney function, reducing stress on the cardiovascular system.
  • Dietary Independence: Continuous cleaning often allows patients to enjoy a less restrictive diet.

Healthcare, Democratized.

We are committed to delivering a fiercely affordable machine and supply ecosystem. Our highly energy-efficient engineering keeps electric bills low, while our cassette-free system allows patients to easily source standard fluid bags from any authorized seller.

Clinical Excellence

Our Advanced Peritoneal Dialysis System and medical-grade consumables are currently undergoing rigorous preparation for human clinical trials in strict accordance with CDSCO Class C and MD-13 regulatory standards.
